# Division template historical names system for Latvia (LAT).
# Immersive Namelists Expanded (INEX)
# Latvian names as the army of 1919-1940 wrote them: an Arabic ordinal with a full stop (1. Kurzemes kājnieku divīzija), lower-case common nouns, place names in the genitive before the noun. Nicknames stand in straight single quotes; the map font has no Latvian quotation marks.
# Overrides all nine vanilla LAT groups (INF, CAV, MOT, MEC, ARM, PAR, MAR, MNT, GAR) and writes over every vanilla number. LAT_INF_01 is the group of the vanilla Ugunskrusts and Baltic Entente focus templates.
