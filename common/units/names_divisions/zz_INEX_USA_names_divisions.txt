# United States (USA) Division Namelist
# Immersive Namelists Expanded (INEX)
#
# American English. Ordered entries carry their ordinal (%dst, %dnd, %drd, %dth) matched to the key;
# fallbacks use the bare number, as vanilla does.
#
# Line and mobile: Infantry (1st-106th, Fortitude deceptions, postwar reserve), National Guard, Regimental Combat Teams,
# Motorized, Mechanized, Cavalry, Armored, Armored Cavalry Regiments, Separate Tank and Tank Destroyer Battalions,
# Field Artillery Brigades, Assault Divisions, Provisional Task Forces.
# Specialists: Airborne, Marines, Marine Raiders, Amtrac Battalions, Mountain, Rangers.
# Territorial: 38 WWII State Guards, State Militias, Harbor Defense Commands, Overseas Garrisons, US Constabulary (1946-52).
# Ideology and alternate history: Red Guards, Lincoln Brigades, Red Armored (communism); Silver Legions, Nationalist Militias,
# Confederate Divisions (fascism); Federal Emergency Forces, Continental Army (neutrality); Union Divisions,
# Conservation Corps (democratic); Volunteer Legions, Expeditionary Forces (democratic or neutrality).
# Tank Destroyer and Field Artillery groups are typed as armor and gated is_ai = no (players only).
#
# Overrides vanilla USA_INF_01, USA_CAV_01, USA_MOT_01, USA_MEC_01, USA_ARM_01, USA_PAR_01, USA_MAR_01,
# USA_MNT_01 and USA_GAR_01; vanilla USA_RGR_01 (Ranger Divisions) stays as it is.
# USA_INF_01 is named by vanilla scripted effects and the Dutch focus tree.
