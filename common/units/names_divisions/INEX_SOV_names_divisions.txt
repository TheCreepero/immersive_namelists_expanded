# Immersive Namelists Expanded: Soviet Union (SOV)
#
# Covers historical and wartime Red Army formations across all combat arms:
# - Infantry: Strelkovye Divizii (Rifle Divisions), Narodnoe Opolcheniye (People's Militia)
# - Mobile: Motostrelkovye Divizii (Motor Rifle), Mekhanizirovannye Divizii (Mechanized)
# - Cavalry: Kavaleriyskie Divizii (Red Army & Red Cossack Cavalry)
# - Armor: Tankovye Divizii, Tankovye Korpusa, Tankovye Brigady
# - Elite & Guards: Gvardeyskie Strelkovye Divizii, Gvardeyskie Mekhanizirovannye Korpusa (1-9), Gvardeyskie Tankovye Korpusa (1-12)
# - Specialized: Vozdushno-Desantnye (Guards Airborne 1-10), Morskaya Pekhota (Naval Infantry), Gornostrelkovye (Mountain), Artilleriyskie (Breakthrough Artillery RVGK), Voiska NKVD, Shtrafnye Chasti (Penal)
#
# Orthography: Standard Russian Latin transliteration. Masculine agreement for 'korpus' (tankovyy, gvardeyskiy).
# Overrides vanilla SOV_INF_01, CAV_01/02, MOT_01, MEC_01, ARM_01-04, PAR_01, MAR_01, MNT_01, GAR_01, GRD_01, GMC_01, GTC_01, PEN_01.
