# Division template historical names system for Lithuania (LIT).
# Immersive Namelists Expanded (INEX)
# Lithuanian names as the interwar army wrote them: an Arabic ordinal with a gendered definite ending (1-oji divizija, 3-iasis pulkas), lower-case common nouns, honorifics in the genitive before the noun. Nicknames stand in straight single quotes; the map font has no Lithuanian quotation marks. Riflemen's Union units keep their Roman numerals.
# Overrides all nine vanilla LIT groups (INF, CAV, MOT, MEC, ARM, PAR, MAR, MNT, GAR) and writes over every vanilla number.
