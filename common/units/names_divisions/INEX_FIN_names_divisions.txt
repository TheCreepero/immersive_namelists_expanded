# Division template historical names system for Finland (FIN).
# Immersive Namelists Expanded (INEX)
#
# Language: Finnish nominative with full diacritics; unit identity in single quotes,
# e.g. "12. Divisioona 'Kollaa'". The Swedish Volunteer Corps (SFK) groups and the
# Swedish-speaking Suojeluskunta districts use Swedish.
# Division numbers follow the 1939-44 field army: numbers that existed carry their
# best-known battle or sector; higher numbers are anchored to Suojeluskunta regions.
#
# Infantry, motorised, armoured and mechanised lists come in pairs: the vanilla tag stays
# a plain, un-nicknamed variant ("%d. Divisioona") and a "(Named)" variant carries the
# identities; each pair shares numbering.
#
# Overrides vanilla FIN_INF_01, FIN_CAV_01, FIN_MOT_01, FIN_ARM_01, FIN_MEC_01,
# FIN_GAR_01, FIN_GAR_02, FIN_MAR_01, FIN_MTN_01 and FIN_PAR_01. Scripted vanilla
# groups (FIN_INF_02, FIN_INF_04, FIN_MIL_01, FIN_MIL_02, FIN_PEN_01) are left to vanilla.
