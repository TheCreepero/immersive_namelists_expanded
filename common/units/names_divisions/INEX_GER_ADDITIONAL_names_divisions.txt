# Division template historical names system for Germany (GER): additional formations.
# Immersive Namelists Expanded (INEX)
#
# Numbered series, specialised units, garrisons and fortresses, Kampfgruppen, militias and
# foreign legions. Period German spelling and 1939-45 exonyms (Reval, Wilna, Kiew, Charkow);
# identities appended to a designation go in single quotes, historical proper names do not.
#
# Numbering: Reserve, Volksgrenadier and Grenadier divisions link to GER_Inf_01 and sit on their
# real numbers (one Heer number series). Luftwaffe field divisions, Flak divisions, battalions,
# brigades, garrisons and militias run their own series.
#
# Ideology gating: Volkssturm, SA and foreign legions are fascist-only; Freikorps (neutrality),
# Reichsbanner (democratic) and Red Front Fighters (communism) cover the other paths.
# Overrides vanilla GER_MIL_01, GER_MIL_02 and GER_MIL_03 (all created by vanilla focuses).
