# INEX - France (FRA) division names
#
# Language: French, nominative case, full diacritics. Ordinals follow the vanilla style (1ère, 2ème, 3ème).
# Overrides vanilla groups: FRA_INF_01 and FRA_ARM_02 (fallback names only), FRA_CAV_01, FRA_MOT_01, FRA_MEC_01,
# FRA_DLC_01, FRA_ARM_01, FRA_ARM_03, FRA_PAR_01, FRA_MAR_01, FRA_MNT_01, FRA_GAR_01, FRA_COL_01.
# Vanilla FRA_RGR_01 is not overridden and keeps falling back to vanilla.
# Ideology-dependent groups are gated with has_government only, never by national focus.
# An ordered key is the division number the name is used for; %d is replaced by that number.
