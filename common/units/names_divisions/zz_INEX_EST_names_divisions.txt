# Division template historical names system for Estonia (EST).
# Immersive Namelists Expanded (INEX)
# Estonian names as the army wrote them: an Arabic ordinal with a full stop (1. jalaväepolk, 7. Eesti laskurdiviis), lower-case common nouns, proper names and Eesti capitalised. Nicknames stand in the nominative in straight single quotes; the map font has no Estonian quotation marks. Period spellings of the 1919 vehicles are kept (Kapten Irw, Wahur, Wibulane, Suuropi).
# Overrides all nine vanilla EST groups (INF, CAV, MOT, MEC, ARM, PAR, MAR, MNT, GAR) and writes over every vanilla number.
