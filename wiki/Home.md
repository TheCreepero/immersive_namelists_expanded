# Immersive Namelists Expanded — Wiki

**Immersive Namelists Expanded (INEX)** is a Hearts of Iron IV mod providing historically plausible, linguistically accurate division namelists for historical and alternate-history campaigns.

## Design Philosophy

INEX prioritizes **historical plausibility over rigid accuracy**. Names are anchored in authentic peacetime mobilization structures, regional conventions, and cultural heritage — then extrapolated to support full wartime campaigns, expanded forces, and alternate-history trajectories without running dry on names.

Key features:
- **Linguistic precision** — native-language diacritics, correct grammatical cases, and proper ordinal formats throughout
- **Gameplay scalability** — 20–50+ ordered entries per group with well-formatted fallback names
- **Coverage breadth** — from frontline divisions to coastal fortresses, territorial defence leagues, elite guards, partisans, and late-war emergency formations
