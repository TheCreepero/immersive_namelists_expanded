## Rationale
- Stripped the vanilla `%dème ...` stubs (identical output via fallback_name) from all overridden groups; INF_01 and ARM_02 became fallback-only plain groups (R10). INF_02 links numbering to INF_01.
- Authored from the verified order of battle: DC/spahis/cavalry brigades, DLC 1-6, DLM 1-4+7, DCR 1-4, DB 1-3+5-6, DIA/DIM alpine and mountain, DIF 101-105 plus Maginot and Alpine sector titles, para (25e DA, 10e/25e DP, 11e DLI), marine base titles plus 9e DIMa.
- INF_02 reduced to 43 entries: kept the sound virtues and branches, added sourced nicknames (11e, 14e, 39e, 66e, 133e, 164e, 5e, 36e) and republican/battle traditions.
- Selectors made plural and shortened; header rewritten; banners and override comments added; dead commented self-links removed.
- Gating: GARDE_NAT, DEF, METRO democratic; new FRA_MIL_FAS_01 (fascism), FRA_MIL_COM_01 (communism), FRA_MIL_NEU_01 (neutrality).
- COL_01: respelled du Tonkin, du Levant; added Senegalese and AOF/AEF titles; numbered DIC placeholders kept.
- Tooling note: -EditNames emitted a doubled CR after adding a new section; normalized to CRLF after each call.
