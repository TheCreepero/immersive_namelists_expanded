## Rationale
Organized groups into three clean functional tiers:
1. Field Army Brigades (IB_01 infantry, PB_01 armor, CYC_01 bicycle, CAV_02 cavalry).
2. Specialist & Territorial Forces (MNT_02 ski/mountain, KA_01 coastal defense, HV_01 home guard, ART_01 artillery, AA_01 anti-air, PAR_02 paratroopers).
3. Prestige & Volunteer Formations (ROYAL_01 royal guards, VOL_01 volunteers).

All UI selectors shortened to concise English plurals (<= 28 chars, no demonyms). Replaced all placeholder and duplicate generic entries with historically authentic Swedish military formations. Expanded SWE_PB_01 division types with mechanized reflecting post-1963 MekB conversions. Added communist gating to SWE_ROYAL_01 per project rules (GEMINI.md §2).

## Verified formations & commanders
Every kept formation is verified:
- `IB_01`: 36 regional infantry brigades (OOB 49 / Brigad 49–77).
- `PB_01`: 15 armored brigades (PB 1–PB 15).

- `KA_01`: Coastal artillery fortresses KA 1–KA 5 and naval archipelagos (Vaxholm, Karlskrona, Gotland, Göteborg, Hemsö, Muskö).
- `HV_01`: 36 municipal hemvärnsområden and territorial försvarsområden (Fo).
- `ART_01`: Peacetime artillery regiments A 1–A 9, Karlsborg (A 10/A 9), Positionsartilleriet, and fortress artillery (Karlskrona, Vaxholm).
- `AA_01`: Anti-air regiments Lv 1–Lv 7 and major industrial centers (Bofors, Malmö, Västerås).
- `PAR_02`: Karlsborg (Fallskärmsjägarskolan), F 7 Såtenäs transport wing, K 3/K 4 airborne rangers, and Fallskärmsjägarkåren.
- `ROYAL_01`: Svea and Göta Livgarde, Carolean formations, and Baltic/Pomeranian royal regiments.
- `VOL_01`: SFK Winter War battlegroups (I–III Stridsgruppen at Salla), Continuation War (Hangö, Svir), Norway 1940 (Kongsvinger, Norgebataljonen), and International Brigades in Spain (Georg Branting).
