## Rationale
- **Linguistic Modernization**: Removed all instances of the ungrammatical Spanglish calque "Rangeros", replacing them with authentic Mexican military terms *Cazadores* and *Guerrilleros*. Fixed the non-standard ordinal abbreviation `11er` in marine battalions to `11º`.
- **Identity Deduplication**: Replaced duplicated entries `General Tomás Mejía` and `Emperatriz Carlota` in `MEX_IMP_ARM_01` with `General Leonardo Márquez` and `Emperador Maximiliano I`, clearing identity conflicts.
- **UI Readability & Concise Selectors**: Shortened `Armored & Motorized Divisions` (30 chars) to `Armored & Mobile Divisions` (26 chars), eliminating UI dropdown truncation. Streamlined `MEX_REG_INF_01` by removing repetitive `(Xª Zona Militar - ...)` prefixes, keeping regional deployment anchors in clean parentheticals `(D.F.)`, `(Baja California)`, etc. Shortened overly long heroic names in `MEX_GEN_INF_02` and shock brigade in `MEX_CD_01`.
- **Numbering Invariants**: Renumbered `MEX_GEN_PAR_01` paratrooper division keys monotonically (5ª-14ª) to prevent units spawning with out-of-sequence ordinal keys.
- **Communist & Agrarian Formations**: Authored `MEX_AGR_01` (20 authored peasant/ejidal defense brigades) and `MEX_RED_01` (20 authored CTM, railway, petroleum, and socialist worker brigades) to provide complete authentic representation for left-wing Mexico.
