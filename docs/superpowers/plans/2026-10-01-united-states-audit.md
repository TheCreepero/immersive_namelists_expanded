## Rationale
The United States is one of the most played nations in Hearts of Iron IV and features extensive military history across European, Pacific, and Mediterranean theaters, as well as multiple distinct alternate-history focus paths in the *Man the Guns* DLC (Communist, Fascist/Confederate, Non-Aligned Military Junta). The current namelist file largely consists of unmodernized vanilla Paradox copy-pastes with sports team jokes, fictional gamey alliterative names, and ungated political groups.

The modernization and expansion plan organizes the US forces into five coherent operational suites:
1. **Regular Army & Mobilized Frontline Forces**:
   - `USA_INF_01`: Infantry Divisions (1st–106th historical divisions, authentic AUS expansion, Fortitude deception units; removal of sports team filler and corrupted vanilla characters).
   - `USA_GAR_01`: National Guard Divisions (state-identified divisions: Yankee, New York, Keystone, Blue and Gray, Old Hickory, Dixie, Red Arrow, Prairie, Red Bull, Santa Fe, Texas, Buckeye, Cyclone, Sunshine, Sunset, Rainbow, Winged Victory, Thunderbird).
   - `USA_ARM_01`: Armored Divisions (1st–16th WWII divisions with nicknames, postwar/NG armored divisions 27th, 30th, 40th, 48th, 49th, 50th, Fortitude units, Combat Commands).
   - `USA_MOT_01` / `USA_MEC_01`: Motorized & Mechanized Divisions (linked numbering with infantry).
   - `USA_CAV_01`: Cavalry Divisions (1st, 2nd, 3rd, 21st, 22nd, 23rd, 24th Cavalry Divisions, separate cavalry brigades/regiments, replacing empty placeholders).
2. **Mobile Reconnaissance & Independent Armored Support (New Groups)**:
   - `USA_ACR_01` (NEW): Armored Cavalry Regiments (2nd, 3rd, 4th, 6th, 11th, 14th ACRs, 106th, 107th, 113th, 115th Cavalry Groups).
   - `USA_TNK_01` (NEW): Separate Tank Battalions (70th, 741st, 743rd, 756th, 761st "Black Panthers", 771st).
   - `USA_TD_01` (NEW): Tank Destroyer Battalions (601st, 701st, 704th, 814th, 823rd).
3. **Specialized, Airborne & Amphibious Forces**:
   - `USA_PAR_01`: Airborne Divisions (11th, 13th, 17th, 82nd, 101st, Fortitude airborne 6th, 9th, 18th, 21st, 135th, 501st–517th PIR, 555th Triple Nickles).
   - `USA_MAR_01`: Marine Divisions (1st–6th historical USMC divisions with official monikers, plus 7th–12th wartime expansion).
   - `USA_MAR_BGD_01` (NEW): Marine Raider & Defense Battalions (1st–6th Provisional Marine Brigades, Carlson's & Edson's Raiders, Paramarines, 1st–18th Marine Defense Battalions).
